StockNews · Desktop screens · v3 · 6 Nov 2026

What happened to what I hold.

Desktop only, one signed-in user, one set of holdings. Two things change from the current build. The sidebar is gone — two destinations never justified 224 px of permanent chrome — and the signal system is rebuilt, because the app now shows prices and news tone on the same screen and those are two different things.

The two signal systems

Unrealised gain — bare mono text

+€6,105.20 −4.3% 0.0%
Above cost--up
Below cost--down

The only coloured figure in the app, and it compares the close to what you paid — never to yesterday. Sign and number carry it; colour only supports. Never boxed, never a chip, always tabular mono, right-aligned in tables.

News tone — chip with the word

Positive Negative Neutral Mixed
Positive--tone-pos
Negative--tone-neg
Neutral--tone-neu
Mixed--tone-mix

Azure and amber, deliberately off the profit/loss axis. Word always present, plus a distinct glyph — so tone survives colour-blindness and never reads as buy/sell.

Everything else is colourless

In your holdings

Buttons, the chart line, the logo mark and avatars are white or neutral. The six semantic hues are the only colour on screen, so they are never competing for attention with the brand.

  • Two destinations only — Home and Holdings. Settings isn’t planned, so there is nothing a rail would hold.
  • Search sits in the bar, reachable from every screen with ⌘K. It both opens an instrument and adds a holding.

Why adding lives here

  • One place writes a holding. Search navigates, the instrument page writes. There is no second add form to keep in step with this one.
  • You see what you are adding — price, chart, exchange and currency are on screen while you type the quantity, so picking the wrong listing is obvious before you commit.
  • The same card edits and removes, so the position has exactly one home. The Holdings table keeps its inline edit for quick number changes.

Search behaviour

  • The bar is a button, not an input. Clicking it or pressing ⌘K opens the modal, which holds the real field already focused.
  • One action per result. ↵ opens the instrument page. No modifier keys, nothing hidden.
  • “Add a security” on Holdings opens the same modal. It is an entry point to search, not a different flow — you still land on the page and add from there.

Cost of the simpler flow

  • Adding is three steps, not two: search, open, add. Deliberate — one add surface instead of two, and the extra step is where you confirm you picked the right listing.
  • Held securities still show a badge in results, so you can tell at a glance whether opening the page will offer Add or Edit.
  • CSV import stays the bulk path. Adding twelve holdings by hand was never the intended route.

Decisions and why

Separating the two signals

  • Unrealised gain is bare mono text, green or red, sign first. It is the only comparison the app makes — close against cost basis, never against the previous close.
  • News tone is a chip in azure, amber, slate or violet — deliberately off the profit/loss axis, so a Negative brief never reads as “sell”.
  • They differ in hue, form and placement, not hue alone. Tone chips sit at the top-right of a brief card; gain figures only ever sit in a numeric column or a position row.
  • Each tone also carries a distinct glyph — filled, outlined, bar, half-filled. Tone survives greyscale and colour-blindness, which the colour-only rule rates High severity.

Layout and type

  • Three faces, three jobs. Newsreader for brief prose, Inter for the interface, JetBrains Mono for every figure, ticker and provenance line.
  • Brief text caps at 74 characters per line even on a wide screen, because a 600-character brief across 1200 px is unreadable.
  • Dense spacing scale (4–32 px) for a dashboard, but the brief card breathes — it is the one thing being read rather than scanned.
  • Numeric columns are right-aligned tabular mono, so decimal points line up down the table.
  • No day change anywhere. There is one close and one cost basis; the app never compares a price to the one before it, so there is no “today” to be wrong about.

Carried over, and what’s new

  • Kept: black canvas, near-black surfaces, dense spacing.
  • Gone: the 224 px sidebar. Home and Holdings sit in the top bar with the search and the account menu, which returns the full width to the table and gives the brief column room to breathe.
  • Changed: the orange and teal accents are gone. With six hues now carrying meaning, a brand hue would be the seventh thing competing — so the accent is contrast instead: white buttons, white chart line, neutral avatars.
  • Follows from that: instrument avatars lost their tints. The monogram was always what told MC from IW; the tint was decoration that would have reintroduced colour.
  • Also: green is reserved for price. The CSV stepper, the “Will import” status and the success banner use neutral white instead, so nothing outside a price reads as a gain.
  • The provenance line is unchanged from the LSEG pattern: article count, window, generation timestamp, in mono, under every brief.
  • Prices never appear without an as-of stamp — in the top bar for the page, next to the figure on an instrument.
  • Prices only exist for securities you hold. An unheld instrument page shows identity and the add card, nothing numeric.
  • Every empty state proposes an action; every error says what to do next rather than apologising.
